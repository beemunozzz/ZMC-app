## What to expect on moving day

Thanks for choosing Zachery's Moving Co.! Here's how your move will go, so there are no surprises.

### Before we arrive
- **Pack and label your boxes.** Write the room name on each box so we know where it goes.
- **Empty drawers and cabinets** [that are fragile or very heavy]. Clothes in sturdy dressers can usually stay.
- **Clear the path.** Make sure walkways, stairs and the driveway are open so we can work safely.
- **Set aside what you're taking yourself:** medicine, keys, important papers, jewelry, cash and phone chargers.
- **Plan for pets and little ones** to be somewhere safe and out of the way.
- **Parking:** save a spot close to the door for the truck. If you live in an apartment or gated community, let us know about gate codes, elevators or loading rules.
- [**Disconnect appliances** like the washer, dryer and fridge before we arrive.]

### The day before
We'll text you a reminder with your arrival time and crew size.

### On moving day
- We'll **text you when we're on our way** with our expected arrival time.
- Your **crew lead** will walk through the home with you, then the crew gets to work.
- We protect your things with **moving blankets** [and shrink wrap], and use **floor runners** on rainy days.
- At the end, the crew lead goes over the final bill with you, and you sign on their phone.

### How pricing works
- **$65 per mover, per hour**, with a **2-hour minimum**.
- **The drive between your two addresses is on the clock.**
- **Trip charge:** the first 25 miles of travel from our shop to you and back are free. Longer trips add a little crew time.
- **Truck:** if we bring our truck, it's **$1 per mile**. If you have your own truck, there's no truck charge.
- **Not-to-exceed price:** California requires a written not-to-exceed price for hourly moves. **Your final bill will never be more than that number.**

### Paying
We accept **cash, check, Zelle, card, Apple Pay, Cash App and Venmo**. Payment is due when the move is done.

### Things we don't move
- **Pianos and hot tubs.** We'll gladly connect you with a trusted specialist.
- [**Hazardous items** like gasoline, propane tanks, paint, pool chemicals and fireworks.]
- [**Plants, food that can spoil, and live animals.**]
